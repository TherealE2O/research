## 2. THE SPECIFIC INVESTIGATION: HUMANGLE'S EXPOSÉ (OCTOBER 2026)

### A. The Protagonists & Their Stories
1. **Kauna Malgwi (Nigerian, Clinical Psychologist & Lead Organizer)**
   - **Background:** Grew up in Borno State, Northeast Nigeria—an area ravaged by Boko Haram insurgency and terror attacks. She moved to Nairobi to pursue her Master’s degree.
   - **The Deception:** In 2019, she was recruited by outsourcing firm **Sama** for what she was told was a "Hausa translation role".
   - **The Horror:** Upon arrival and after signing strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), she discovered her actual job was moderating graphic content for Meta (Facebook/Instagram). She was forced to review raw footage of Boko Haram executions, bandit massacres, beheadings, and torture from her own homeland.
   - **The Quote/Impact:** Moderating violence from the very insurgency she fled triggered deep trauma. She describes experiencing persistent insomnia, panic attacks, and severe psychological distress.
   - **The Resistance:** After being discarded in 2023, Malgwi founded the **Digital Rights and Mental Health Initiative Africa (DRMHI)** and became a lead organizer in the **African Content Moderators Union**.

2. **Mustafa Mukhtar (Nigerian Recruit)**
   - **The Deception:** Recruited from Nigeria under the belief that he was taking on customer care and linguistic translation work.
   - **The Reality:** Placed in front of screens showing relentless horrors—suicides, sexual violence, and graphic murders.
   - **The Inadequacy of Counseling:** Mukhtar revealed that the in-house "wellness counselors" provided by the company were so overwhelmed and undertrained that **the therapists themselves broke down in tears** when moderators described the videos they had just processed.

### B. The Factory Conditions: The 50-Second Death Clock
- **The KPI Quota:** Moderators were forced to watch, analyze, and make high-stakes policy decisions on traumatizing videos within an arbitrary **50-second limit** per ticket.
- **Surveillance & Penalties:** Every second of their work was monitored by keystroke loggers and automated timers. If their accuracy dropped below 95%—even while viewing child sexual abuse material or dismemberments—they were reprimanded or threatened with termination.
- **Poverty Wages:** Nigerian and African moderators were paid between **40,000 to 60,000 Kenyan Shillings per month (~$300 to $450 USD)**. That breaks down to roughly **$1.50 to $2.20 per hour**—making them the lowest-paid Facebook moderators on planet earth.

## 4. SIMILAR & EXPANDING SCANDALS (THE CONNECTED CASES)

To make the video rich, authoritative, and systemic, the writer should connect the HumAngle story to these 3 major parallel investigations:

### Case 1: The OpenAI / ChatGPT Toxic Data Labeling Scandal (Time Magazine)
- **The Backstory:** In 2021-2022, before ChatGPT stunned the world with its launch, OpenAI had a massive problem: raw internet text was filled with hate speech, pedophilia, bestiality, suicide, and sexual violence.
- **The African Exploitation:** OpenAI hired **Sama in Nairobi** to read, categorize, and tag hundreds of thousands of toxic text snippets so that AI safety guardrails could be built.
- **The Pay:** Kenyan data labelers were paid between **$1.32 and $2.00 per hour** to read horrific descriptions of sexual abuse and suicide for hours every day.
- **The Outcome:** Workers suffered acute depression and recurring flashbacks. Sama terminated the contract with OpenAI early because the content was deemed too psychologically toxic for human beings.

### Case 2: The Tigray War & The Murder of Professor Meareg Amare ($2 Billion Lawsuit)
- **The Backstory:** During the brutal civil war in Ethiopia (2020-2022), Facebook became a primary vehicle for ethnic hate speech, doxxing, and incitement to murder.
- **The Understaffed Nairobi Hub:** Meta assigned fewer than **30 content moderators** to cover over 100 million people across Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa speaking Amharic, Oromo, and Tigrinya. The moderators were hopelessly overwhelmed.
- **The Tragedy:** In 2021, Professor Meareg Amare, a respected chemistry academic at Bahir Dar University, was targeted by viral Facebook posts falsely accusing him of treason, along with his home address. Despite repeated desperate reports to Facebook by his son Abrham Meareg, Meta’s systems failed to remove the posts.
- **The Result:** Gunmen followed Professor Meareg home and murdered him on his doorstep.
- **The Lawsuit:** Abrham Meareg and human rights groups filed a **$2 Billion (250 billion KSh)** lawsuit against Meta in Kenya High Court for algorithmically promoting hate speech that cost African lives.

### Case 3: Whistleblower Daniel Motaung (South Africa) & Union Busting
- **The Pioneer:** Daniel Motaung, a top university graduate from South Africa, was recruited in 2019 to Nairobi for what he thought was an administrative role.
- **The Breakdown:** Exposed to continuous live streams of beheadings and child abuse. He was diagnosed with severe clinical PTSD.
- **The Retaliation:** When Motaung tried to organize an internal union to demand living wages and independent psychiatric care, Sama immediately fired him and had his work permit threatened.
- **The Trial:** Motaung filed Petition No. E070 of 2022 in Kenya, sparking the entire legal domino effect.

## 5. THE TURNING POINT: COURT VICTORIES IN NAIROBI (2024–2026)

This is the climax of the story: **Africa is fighting back and winning.**

1. **Court of Appeal Ruling (September 2024):**
   - The Kenyan Court of Appeal delivered a historic blow to Big Tech: It ruled that **Meta CAN be sued in Kenyan courts**. The judge dismissed Meta's claim of corporate immunity, establishing that if you profit from African users and exploit African labor, you answer to African law.
2. **Psychiatric Evidence Filed (December 2024):**
   - Official psychiatric evaluations of 144 moderators conducted by independent medical experts were submitted to the Kenyan court: **81% of moderators were diagnosed with severe clinical PTSD**, with massive rates of chronic depression, anxiety disorders, and insomnia.
3. **Executive Subpoena (April 2026):**
   - The court ordered senior Meta executives to appear for cross-examination regarding company claims and internal affidavits.
4. **The $1.6 Billion Compensation Claim:**
   - The moderators, represented by legal teams including Nzili and Sumbi Advocates and supported by UK-based justice non-profit **Foxglove**, are demanding $1.6 Billion in healthcare funds, restitution, and compensation for lost mental health.
